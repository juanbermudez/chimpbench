**Lean-season starvation (5 October 2026).** S39 starves at 12 months (Part C); the walk-back put the cause before S27. **E1r** (merged a9739c3, `docs/staging/e1r-prereg.md`): in the lean season the small and reproducing females' foregut is full, and a valuation trap (`forageRate` prices a crown by the gut's room in drupe units, the fallback at its full rate) keeps depleted animals eating the food the gut passes least energy from. **E1s** (`gutValue`, merged 1c1bcb2 with the switch at 0; `docs/staging/e1s-prereg.md` §8.8): stopped after 3 iterations, none passing.
