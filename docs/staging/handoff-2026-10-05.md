## 0. In one minute

- **The project.** ChimpBench (formerly MGOGO) is a deterministic 3D simulation of an eastern chimpanzee society
  (Kibale-like, field profile 8 km) and a demo of a local decision model (GLiNER2.5-Decide) choosing chimp actions.
  Behaviour is scored by a virtual field observer against 150 cited field targets (`data/targets.json`).
- **Track E ("Emergence")** rebuilds decisions so behaviour comes from physiology, perception and memory (an energy
  ledger with a gut, sleep pressure, a circadian clock, hormone-like states, beliefs) instead of prescribed timers,
  dice and bonuses. Every stage ships behind a switch that is 0 by default; field numbers are targets, never inputs.
- **Best stack: S39** (56 switch settings, §4.1): 42 prescriptions against 147 for today's model (105 replaced). It
  beats today's model on the new bands at 60 days, 6 and 12 months, is about even on rows whose bands did not change,
  is worse on the old bands, and is **not viable at 12 months**: 6 starvation deaths in 20 seed-runs.
- **The open problem** is that starvation. Decision-side fixes failed (E1s stopped; E1t not kept). The gut audit
  (E1u) shows the lean-season limit rests on gut inputs nobody has measured; wild chimpanzees survive lean seasons,
  so a mechanism is missing, most likely **wadging** (chewing pith and spitting out the fibre; never weighed).
- **The user's latest decision:** test wadging as a range (stage **E1v**, registered at 137d5aa). **Its
  implementation has not started**: the agent was stopped by the account's weekly usage limit (resets **10 October,
  8 pm America/New_York**). Nothing else is running.
- **Your first three actions:** (1) implement E1v as registered (§6.1); (2) run its two arms and report starvation per
  arm (§6.1); (3) ask the user the pending UI question (§7.1) if still open.

Gotchas: use `/usr/bin/python3` (Homebrew's 3.14 lacks pyexpat, numpy); `tsc --noEmit -p .` checks `src/` only;
background jobs die after 2 hours (the runner's budget handles it); the machine (12 cores) is shared with other sessions
and load swings from 5 to 30 (spikes to 160–300 seen), slowing seeds 2–3×; disk was 11 GB free at 19:30 (the runner
needs 5 GB plus outputs; a 12-month run's checkpoints are about 35 MB per seed); respiratory outbreaks kill 5–13
animals in some runs, so check causes before blaming a switch; at 6 months births are only the 10 founder pregnancies,
so "births ≥ deaths" there cannot separate arms (e1t-prereg.md §7 amendment).

Merging a stage into `track-e`: from the `track-e` worktree only, never while a run uses it; `git merge --no-ff`;
expected conflicts in `src/sim/candidates.ts` (keep both), `tests/sim-track-e.test.ts` and `scripts/lib/prescriptions.ts`
(union of switch lists), `src/sim/params.gen.ts` (take ours, regenerate), `docs/research.md` (keep both sections);
then `gen-params --check`, `tsc`, `pnpm test` (0 fail), `decision-guide --check`, commit, and
`git ls-files data/raw node_modules` must print nothing.

Agent prompts: the templates are the `*-prompt.txt` files in the scratch copy and the pre-flight block in
`track-e-handoff.md` §7. Every prompt names the worktree, the files to read, the registration to follow, the hard
rules (privacy, Unpaywall, bot checks, seeds, load), the frozen-checkout rule and a word limit for the report.
