## 0. The question

E2d entered process S (rise τ 18.2 h, decay τ 4.2 h; thresholds 0.67 / 0.17, amplitude 0.12) and process C (the
forger1999 pacemaker, τ 24.2 h) with human values, tagged *assumed*. On the rhythm reference (`rhythmSleep`,
`rhythmHeat`, `departRace`, `nestLightDecide`, `rhythmCircadian`) sleep runs from ~100 min after sunset to ~128 min
before sunrise (8.2 h); adults are held by the night menu until −13 min and leave (99% before sunrise); independent
5–8-year-olds, whom no menu filters, are out of the nest 96% of the two pre-dawn hours. Is the sleep window wrong for
chimpanzees, and does a sourced chimpanzee window fix the pre-dawn problem without a holder (`rhythmDarkW` or the
night menu)?

## 2. Which numbers may be inputs (the argument)

To be completed with the source table. Principle registered now: a number is an input only if it is a property of the
sleep system measured where the morning's options do not decide it: amount of sleep and its architecture by EEG (or
validated video/actigraphy scoring of sleep, not of leaving), under natural light; a time of *leaving* the sleeping
place (captive or wild) is behaviour and stays a target. No input is chosen, scaled or interpolated to reach a
departure time, a waking time or a pre-dawn share.
