to 2.97 km a day and removes the juveniles' energy deficit (−0.148 → +0.004%/day). E4c's confirm showed calls raising
party size (T-PTY-1 3.57 → 3.84) and intergroup contacts (T-IGE-1 3.4 → 7.5).
